Privacy Policy

Sweep · Last updated 29 October 2026

Sweep doesn't collect any data.

Sweep reads your photo library only to show your photos and videos on your iPhone, so you can choose which ones to delete. Your photos are never uploaded, copied off the device, or shared with anyone, including the developer.

The choices you make (which photos you kept or put in the Bin) and your cleanup totals are stored only on your iPhone, inside the app. Deleting the app removes them.

Sweep has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it doesn't connect to the internet.

Similar-photo detection uses Apple's Vision framework, on your iPhone.
