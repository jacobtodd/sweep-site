Common questions

Does swiping left delete a photo?
No. It goes into Sweep's Bin. Nothing is deleted until you open the Bin and tap Delete, and iOS asks you to confirm.
I deleted something by mistake.
Open the Photos app, go to Albums › Recently Deleted, and recover it. Deleted items stay there for 30 days.
Sweep only shows some of my photos.
You probably allowed access to selected photos only. Go to Settings › Privacy & Security › Photos › Sweep and choose Full Access.
Is there a subscription?
No. Sweep is a one-time purchase, with no in-app purchases, ads or limits.
Are my photos uploaded anywhere?
No. Everything happens on your iPhone. See the privacy policy.
